<p class="cs-section">Highlights</p>

<div class="cs-cards">
<div class="cs-card"><div class="cs-card-num">01</div><h3>ISO week conversions</h3><p>Convert between date, ISO year, ISO week and ISO yearweek. The suffix says what comes back: <code>_c</code> a character string, <code>_n</code> a number.</p></div>
<div class="cs-card"><div class="cs-card-num">02</div><h3>Season week</h3><p>Convert between ISO week and season week. Season week numbers the weeks within an epidemiological season, so influenza and other seasonal data line up across years.</p></div>
<div class="cs-card"><div class="cs-card-num">03</div><h3>Bundled calendars</h3><p>Lookup tables give the dates within each ISO yearweek. Two more tables give Norwegian public holidays and workday counts, by date and by ISO yearweek. All three are ready to join into a surveillance dataset.</p></div>
</div>
